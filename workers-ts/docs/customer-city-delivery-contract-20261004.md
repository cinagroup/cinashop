## Wire and outcome

The existing customer delivery endpoint selects `city_delivery` or `city_split_delivery` with exact payload:

```json
{"type":2,"delivery_type":2,"station_type":1,"cargo_weight":"2.50","delivery_remark":"配送备注"}
```

`station_type` is 1 (Dada) or 2 (UU). The weight is positive and at most 100 kilograms, at most two decimal places. Remarks are at most 255 characters and contain no control characters. Split adds original `cart_ids:[{"cart_id":"cart-1","cart_num":1}]`; the shared adapter converts these to the Supplier engine's internal cart names. The outer operation body also binds version, current scope key, physical order ID and both observed order/fulfillment revisions. The original v4 UUID and canonical hash are immutable.

The outer receipt is `provider-admitted`: it records durable admission, not delivery success. It binds the real `city_job_id`. Post-commit dispatch may advance the job; clients query the dynamic job instead of treating the original PENDING evidence as current progress. The job outcome has only job_id, request_key, status, provider, provider_order_id, delivery_status, last_error_code, updated_at and may_retry=false. It contains no station, recipient, raw provider response or credentials.

`GET /api/mobile/work/operations/city-jobs/:id` uses ordinary User authentication and only the owning actor. After customer-role loss, the owning current ordinary session can still read this minimal factual outcome. Password/account deletion/status/expiry revocation still rejects. `POST /api/mobile/work/operations/city-jobs/:id/recover` requires the exact original `{request_key}` and its matching operation receipt plus fresh customer authority. Recovery queries the original provider identity; it does not publish a second shipment or replace the original body/UUID.

## Durable state and authority

Dada's job provider_order_id is additionally checked against the frozen intent origin. Before UNKNOWN recovery makes any external query, the saved quote's provider origin must also equal that same identity. A column update allowed for UU's actual returned origin cannot cause Dada to query another entity. The native negative uses ordinary LOGIN to change job origin and saved quote independently and proves zero recovery fetches and zero local fulfillment.

`customer_city_delivery_job` persists original actor UID, service, password auth version, expiry, scope, UUID/hash, requested/root/physical/customer/store/supplier identity and canonical intent. `customer_city_delivery_attempt` provides one exact phase per job. `customer_city_delivery_binding` names the exact job/attempt/store_delivery_order/order/root/customer/store/supplier/provider ID and active flag. Callback projection does not select the newest historical delivery row.

Admission acquires the reviewed table fence before root/order locks. Processing uses the same graph, a 60-second lease, and one durable attempt. PENDING moves to PROCESSING; ISSUING is persisted before external create. Confirmed acceptance plus actual returned provider data can become ADMITTED after genuine local fulfillment. Known negative provider replies become REJECTED. Transport/malformed/contradictory replies, expired issued leases or acceptance followed by a local apply failure remain UNKNOWN. Scheduled dispatch compensates genuinely pending work and never republishes an ambiguous issued attempt.

Both the queued claim and the post-quote preflight revalidate the current actor, immutable physical-order/cart fingerprint, current provider readiness and the exact actual station snapshot. Disabling or changing registration/address/phone while awaiting quote stops create. A private HMAC binds current resolved credentials, callback origin and commissioning setting; public configured readiness alone cannot hide a deployment credential change. This HMAC and raw intent remain server-only.

The shared city fence blocks other manual/electronic fulfillment while PENDING, PROCESSING, UNKNOWN or active ADMITTED exists. The matching job exemption is supplied by the city service itself. Cancellation retires the active binding; a later new UUID is a new factual attempt. Old callbacks cannot complete the new attempt. Delivered callbacks use real order receipt settlement/rewards once. Legacy callbacks with no durable binding retain the strict single-historical-attempt rule and reject ambiguity.

City notices have `order.delivery.notice:<physicalId>:city:<jobId>` and a mandatory job ID. Whole order, split child and split-all-quantity fallback propagate that job. Old outbox payloads remain immutable. Before notice generation and before a queued provider notification begins, the shared notifier verifies the actual active binding, accepted attempt, provider delivery record and current physical order. A rider callback may replace order.deliveryId with rider phone; provider authority remains the durable delivery record. A cancelled previously ENQUEUED notice is SKIPPED without a fetch. Only delivery notices accept the positive city suffix; refund/withdrawal/pink event grammars do not expand.
